FlashExit demo narration (English, target under 3 minutes, ~330 words)

[0:00 - title]
FlashExit. A cross-chain stop-loss that fires itself, in seconds.

[0:08 - the problem]
If your tokens live on one chain and the price moves on another,
a stop-loss is only as fast as you are. Keepers poll. Humans sleep.
By the time anyone reacts, the exit price is gone.

[0:24 - the idea]
FlashExit removes the middleman. A reactive contract on Lasna watches
Uniswap v4 swap events on Base Sepolia directly. When the price crosses
your trigger, the network itself fires a callback that sells on
Unichain Sepolia. No keeper. No polling. No wallet online.

[0:44 - the order]
Here is the order: sell one hundred FLASH if the price falls eight
percent below the pool price. A minimum output guards against slippage,
and a deadline makes the order expire on its own. The tokens are
already deposited, so the exit needs no approval at trigger time.

[1:04 - the trigger]
Now the trigger. A large swap pushes the Base pool price down
fifteen percent, far past the trigger.

[1:14 - the reaction]
Watch Lasna. The reactive contract sees the swap event, marks the
order triggered, and requests the callback.

[1:24 - the exit]
And on Unichain Sepolia, the executor runs the swap and sends the
proceeds straight to the user. From trigger to exit: measured live,
in the table below.

[1:40 - the safeguards]
Three safeguards. One: each order fires exactly once, even in a
volatile pool. Two: if the output falls below your minimum, the
callback reverts and your deposit stays untouched. Three: expired
orders are skipped, never executed.

[2:00 - take-profit]
It works both ways. A take-profit order fires when the price rises
past its trigger, same flow, same speed.

[2:12 - close]
FlashExit: set the trigger, walk away, and let the network watch
the market for you. Built for the Reactive Omni Buildathon,
Track One: Every Second Counts.
